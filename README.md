## How to avoid missing Walmart cashback

Most cashback problems are tracking problems rather than complicated account problems. ShopBack uses the referral click and browser or app session to connect the Walmart order to your account. If that connection breaks, the merchant may not attribute the sale to ShopBack.

Before clicking through to Walmart, take these precautions:

### Start with an empty or nearly empty cart

Adding products to a Walmart cart before activating cashback can sometimes interfere with tracking, depending on the merchant’s conditions. The safest workflow is to review the offer first, activate it, and then add products during the same session.

### Disable ad blockers for the session

ShopBack identifies ad blockers, disabled cookies, private browsing, and browser changes as common reasons cashback fails to track. Allow the relevant cookies and avoid privacy settings that block affiliate attribution during checkout.

### Do not switch devices

Starting on your phone and finishing on a desktop, or beginning in a browser and completing checkout in the Walmart app, can break the tracking chain. ShopBack recommends completing the purchase in the same session.

### Avoid other cashback portals

Visiting another cashback or coupon site after activating ShopBack can replace the referral information. If ShopBack is the cashback service you want to use, keep that session isolated until the order is finished.

### Use approved codes only

An outside code may appear to work at checkout while still making the transaction ineligible for cashback. Check the ShopBack Walmart page for any approved offer before using a code.

### Keep your confirmation email

Save the Walmart order number, purchase date, itemized total, and screenshots of the ShopBack offer if the reward is important. If the cashback does not appear, those details are useful when contacting support.
